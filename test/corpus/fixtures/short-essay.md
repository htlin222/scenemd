# Why documents beat decks

Most presentations begin life as documents and are then flattened into slides, losing the structure that made them readable.

A document-first engine keeps the prose canonical and derives the presentation from the structure that already exists.

## The cost of flattening

Slide tools force early spatial commitment. Every sentence must be assigned to a rectangle before the argument is finished.

## What structure gives back

Headings carry hierarchy, paragraphs carry argument, and lists carry enumeration. All three survive in ordinary Markdown.
